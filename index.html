Tennis Tonight Straight-Sets +EV System

How the system works (the 4 filters) and its limits

This tool screens favorites for the "win in straight sets" angle using a strict four-filter system. A match must pass all three data filters to be flagged QUALIFIES; the fourth filter is the value check you run against the book's price.

  • Filter 1 — Ranking gap 50+: the favorite is ranked at least 50 spots above the underdog (current ATP/WTA rankings). Smaller gaps carry too much variance.
  • Filter 2 — Surface mastery 70%+: the favorite's win rate on this match's surface is 70% or higher. Here it's computed over the last 6 seasons of completed matches (a "career" proxy — true lifetime data isn't freely available). Under 70% is an automatic skip.
  • Filter 3 — Recent form 8-2 or better: the favorite won at least 8 of their last 10 completed matches (any surface), confirming health and rhythm.
  • Filter 4 — The edge: matches passing 1–3 hit straight sets ~88% of the time per the system (fair odds ≈ -730). Books often price these markets -300 to -500 (implied 75–83%). Enter the book's straight-set (or set-handicap) American odds on a qualifying card and the tool computes your +EV edge.

Coverage: ESPN's tennis feed carries the main ATP/WTA tour and Grand Slams only — Challenger and ITF events aren't included.

Known limitations: surface win% uses a multi-season window, not true career; rankings beyond the published list are treated as "unranked" (which only widens a gap, never qualifies a bad one); form counts only completed singles matches in the dataset; the 88% true-probability figure is the system author's tracked claim, not independently backtested here; no live odds feed (you enter the price). Treat output as a screening tool, not betting advice.
